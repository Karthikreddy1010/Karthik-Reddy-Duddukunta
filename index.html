Background & Education

I'm a Data Science graduate student and Research Assistant at the Clean Energy and Sustainability Analytics Center (CESAC) at Montclair State University. My work sits at the intersection of predictive analytics, machine learning, and sustainability science.

I specialize in building end-to-end ML systems — from ingestion and feature engineering to model deployment and stakeholder communication — applied across workforce planning, climate-driven energy patterns, and environmental AI at scale (10M+ records).

"Turning messy institutional data into decisions that matter — for energy, environment, and people."

My current research spans deep learning for environmental monitoring and multimodal AI systems — from causal inference on energy pricing behavior to computer vision pipelines for infrastructure risk screening, and the annotation tooling that makes those datasets possible — alongside peer-reviewed work in climate hazard modeling and healthcare AI.

Beyond research, I mentor 10+ high school and master's students, lead five project teams as technical lead, and represent CESAC at policy and industry conferences.

Utility Pole & Wire Detection — Computer Vision Pipeline
2025 – Dec 2025
Independent Research Project · YOLOv8-OBB, DINO, SAM, Active Learning
  • Developed a research pipeline for utility/electric pole detection and geometric characterization from high-resolution street-level imagery using YOLOv8 Oriented Bounding Boxes (OBB), achieving mAP@50 = 0.95, Precision = 0.92, Recall = 0.90.
  • Chose oriented over axis-aligned boxes deliberately: a pole is thin and often tilted, so an axis-aligned box is mostly background and its IoU destabilises precisely as the lean — the quantity being measured — increases.
  • Estimated pole height and leaning angle from OBB major-axis orientation and image metadata, turning each detection into a physical measurement that thresholds into an automated structural-risk flag and a prioritised inspection queue.
  • Designed an automated annotation and quality-control workflow integrating foundation-model object discovery (Grounding DINO / SAM) to reduce manual labeling effort and scale dataset construction — later built out into the PoleAnnotator AI platform.
  • Built an active learning framework with tiled/sliced inference at native resolution, addressing the dominant recall failure mode: small, distant poles that whole-image downsampling erases entirely.
  • Analysed failure modes specific to mobile and Street View imagery — foliage and vehicle occlusion, frame-edge truncation, and thin low-contrast structures against bright sky — rather than reporting aggregate metrics alone.
